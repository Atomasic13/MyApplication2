Process

Brief. Design. Deliver.

No standups, no status calls, no waiting until Friday. The loop runs until the work is done.

i. Send the brief A document, a forwarded email, a voice note — any form will do. DECA turns it into a structured scope with deliverables, timeline, and the questions worth asking.
ii. The studio works Research, direction, production, and revision run as one continuous process across every deliverable in the scope. You're consulted only when a decision is truly yours.
iii. Files arrive Everything exported, named properly, and sent where you already work — Figma, email, Telegram, WhatsApp. No portals, no download links that expire.
